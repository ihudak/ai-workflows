# QUESTION-BUDGET — a question budget for the BRD route — the rulings

**Plan:** `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-26-question-budget.md`. **Design:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-question-budget-design.md`, its status line and its decisions D1 to D16. **Record:** `2026-11-question-budget.md`, beside this file. **Ledger:** the controller's `progress.md` in the build's workspace, `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-26-question-budget/`, which is git-ignored and lost when the branch finishes. The *Line* column below, and every line this file cites, is that ledger's own line. **The ledger is not copied into the repository**, unlike the precedent this file follows (`2026-11-17-scan-docs-paths-rulings.md`): it records a local measurement on a real engagement whose data is private, so only the rulings are kept, and the line numbers point at a file that will not survive the workspace.

A ruling is a decision the controller took on the maintainer's behalf instead of stopping to ask. Every ruling of the build is a ledger line beginning `- Ruling`, or a `Final: parked` line carrying `— Ruling:`; there are seventy-six, listed in ledger order (so the ledger's T12-a, written during Task 12's preparation, stands before T11-a). Each is listed with what was decided, its reason, and what it costs if wrong, so the maintainer can overturn it. The *Ruling* and *What was decided* cells give the ledger's own words, split between the *What was decided* and *Why* cells; nothing in them is softened, merged or dropped. Where the ledger gives no reason, the *Why* cell says so. Where the ledger states no cost, the *Cost if wrong* cell says so and, where the ledger's neighbouring lines allow, gives the cost as read here. Where a later ruling supersedes or amends an earlier one, both rows say so. The workspace's reports and reviews (`task-N-report.md`, `task-N-review.md`, `final-review-A.md`, `-B.md`, `-C.md`, `final-fix-report.md`, `final-rereview.md`, `residual-fix-report.md`, `goal9-score.md`) are in that same workspace and are lost with it; a sentence drawn from one is marked *(from <file>)*.

Abbreviations: T*n* is Task *n* of the plan, F the final whole-branch review and its fix waves, R the pre-flight scan's rulings. D*n* is a decision of the design. *Registry* is the question registry written by `plugins/product-workflows/scripts/question-registry.py`; *rulebook* is `plugins/workflows-core/references/question-budget.md`; *planner* and *applier* are the `question-planner` and `answer-applier` agents. NB*n*, A*n*, B*n*, C*n* are findings of the final review and its re-review. [V] and [C] are the verifier-facing (delivery) and customer-facing questions of the route.
