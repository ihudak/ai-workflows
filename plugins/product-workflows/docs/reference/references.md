# References reference

`product-workflows` bundles 12 files under `references/`, all top-level markdown, no bundled subtrees. This page enumerates every one, grouped by concern below.

The shared corpus every plugin in this family reads — the addressing grammar, the git and phase-handoff entry points, model routing, escalation and triage, cost/feedback/follow-up emission, the grounding and grilling conventions, and the PRD format — ships in the companion `workflows-core` plugin and is enumerated on its own references page, reached through the loader skill rather than by path. What is listed here is what `product-workflows` itself carries.

## BRD-to-PRD route formats

The canonical structure each BRD-route artifact is authored and reviewed against.

- `brd-format.md` — what a BRD is (customer-supplied, immutable once intaken, markdown only), what `brd/source/` and `brd/source-external/` hold beside the document itself — every file it links that the capture took, inside its own directory at the same relative paths and outside it by basename — the `brd/brd-link-log.md` that names every link in a captured file whose target the copy did not capture, maps each captured link that does not resolve as written, and says which file in the directory the document is, the `brd/brd-figures.md` transcription of every linked image the run takes (§1.2), the `kind:`/`key:` header every inventory opens with — carried there and never in the immutable source — with the record a source-owning BRD's inventory keeps of what its rows were reconciled against and of the operator's account of each section holding no row and each image that neither yields nor illustrates one, the link file a slice carries and the one spelling of its `claims:` list, the `[BR#n]` inventory row shape and the split-on-multiple-obligations rule, the six `[DEF#n]` defect classes, and defect-resolution states; the fixed layout of the link log, the inventory and the defect log, and the one cell encoding (`\|` for a literal `|`, `<br>` for a line break) those three share with the coverage ledger; `/brd-intake` authors against it, `brd-reader` extracts against it and `figure-reader` transcribes against its §1.2, `/prd-ground`, `/brd-split` and `/brd-interview` read its defect classes and resolution states, `/brd-package` resolves a slice's ids one hop up and ships the customer's captured files by it, and `/brd-reconcile` writes the `customer-amended <SLICE-KEY> <date>`, `withdrawn` and `resolved-by: <SLICE-KEY>/[CD#n]` resolutions a returned review settles — into the parent's log when it stands on a slice, the first and the last qualified by the slice's key because each slice keeps its own reviews and numbers its own `[CD#n]`.
- `coverage-ledger-format.md` — the coverage ledger's row shape and fixed file layout, its six dispositions (only `unallocated` blocks), the allocation gate `/brd-split` enforces, the D23 roll-up behind its ledger line, the PRD-eligibility rule read from it — satisfiable only on a slice, since a parent BRD is a container whose walk is never offered `covered-here` — the positive test that separates a legacy unprefixed root BRD from a legacy idea-route PRD folder, shared by every command that tests a folder's level (its §5.1 names them), the three refusals an offer naming `/create-prd` has to clear before it names it, what `covered-by` means downstream — a row another BRD owns leaves this BRD's scope as a subject, so neither its interview nor its package puts it to a human twice — and the **sibling re-cut**: what makes a row movable (the parent reads `covered-by: <A>` while A's own ledger reads `deferred-to: <A>`), the two ledger writes and their order, who may receive one, what happens where the receiver is later removed, and the three things it does not relax — no row returns to `unallocated`, the allocation gate never reopens, and the one-level nesting cap is untouched; `/brd-intake` writes the ledger against it, `/prd-ground` and `/brd-split` read and update it, and `/brd-reconcile` moves a row on a frozen customer decision without ever allocating one.
- `decision-register-format.md` — the decision register's record shape for a `[VD#n]` (delivery-team) and `[CD#n]` (customer) decision, its five statuses (including the first-class `withdrawn`), the mandatory `argumentation` field, the two causes that may reopen a decision — and why that rule is what closes a sibling holding decisions to a re-pointed row — what `conditional_on` records and makes findable, the D19 rule against resting solely on a `will-change` finding, and the `[AS#n]` assumption every open one of which reaches the customer, and the **torn write** — an item stamped with a round whose record does not exist or does not name it, which no reader counts and only `/brd-interview` removes; `/brd-interview` writes `[VD#n]` and `[AS#n]` records against it, enforces its will-change rule, and reopens a `decided` `[VD#n]` or `[CD#n]` a re-grounding moved, `/create-prd` on the BRD route writes an `[AS#n]` for a gap only the customer can settle, `brd-package-reviewer` reads them, `/brd-package` surfaces every open assumption and every `conditional_on` position to the customer, and `/brd-reconcile` mints the `[CD#n]` records — the only command that does — holding one conditional or open where the will-change rule fires on it, supersedes the assumptions they settle, and sweeps every `conditional_on` position in every dependent BRD.
- `code-defect-log-format.md` — the code-defect log's record shape: the `[CDF#n]` entry, the rule that every one of them cites a verified `[CG#n]` for the behaviour and names its intent basis separately because grounding adjudicates a claim and has no authority over the code's own intent, the two shapes an `intent_basis` takes (a pointer into a repository or document, or an explicit `operator-judgment` with its reasoning), the five dispositions and why there is no `fixed` one on a route that builds nothing, and the two spellings `blocked_on` fixes so a scope condition that cannot be settled yet does not have to be invented as a decision; `/brd-interview` is its only writer, `brd-package-reviewer` reads it, as do `/brd-package`'s Phase 0 gate, `/brd-reconcile`, `proposal-reviewer` and `/prd-proposal`'s defect sweep, and `/brd-package` ships it to the customer because a defect disposed `in-scope` is the delivery boundary rather than delivery-side bookkeeping.
- `interview-tagging.md` — the interview's three question tags (`[G]` answered from grounding by nobody, `[V]` decided by the delivery team with argumentation, `[C]` put to the customer via the review package), the rule each tag fixes about who may answer, the re-tagging of a `[G]` grounding cannot settle, the split of a question carrying two tags, and the rule that closes a round; `/brd-interview` tags every question against it, `brd-package-reviewer` applies its test to one already routed, `/brd-package` routes the `[C]` questions into the customer prompt and gates on its round vocabulary, and `/brd-reconcile` writes the terminal disposition *answered by the customer* that finally closes a round holding one.
- `bundle-packaging.md` — how the customer-facing bundle is built plugin-free (D12): the four rules that keep it followable by a reviewer with nothing installed, including locating documents by filename search rather than by path; the de-Obsidianising pass that rewrites wikilinks to plain filename references and keeps callouts; the three degradation tiers and what each obliges a returned review to state about its own evidence; the **allow-list of what the bundle may contain** — derived from the rule that a document reaches it only where a prompt part sends the reviewer to it, with the self-review and the delivery note excluded by name — and the customer's own files — the source document and every markdown file captured with it — copied byte for byte rather than rendered, since a rendering pass would break their `source_anchor` traceability; the delivery note's 200-word ceiling and the two facts it must carry; the citation-resolution check that, once the bundle is assembled, verifies every identifier reference resolves against the right source package's own corpus and every bare filename names a document actually in the bundle, with its `[SR#n]` exemption and the rule that it never edits or renames the customer's own content or locators and never stops on them by itself — every hit is reported grouped, one line per file and token, and the operator rules once per pass on the whole report, a ruling whose other option holds the package; the set-resolution check that then compares each restated set to its source by membership rather than by count — a part's enumerated identifiers against its own filter, the manifest against the bundle's review documents, the delivery note's repositories and pins against the bundle's baselines — every relation narrowed against assembled packages, since the obvious form of each fires on a correct bundle; and the `bundle-<YYYYMMDD>/` directory (D18) that, once handed off, is the permanent record of what was sent. `/brd-package` builds every bundle against it, and `/brd-reconcile` reads a review returned against one — and cites the same section for why nothing inside that committed directory is ever bannered or rewritten afterwards.
- `customer-review-schema.md` — the twelve sections the customer's reviewer fills and sends back, the rule that puts the review's evidence limitations ahead of every claim resting on them, and the D13 rule that the review is exactly one new file with nothing in the package modified; unlike every other reference here its body is rendered verbatim into the customer's prompt, so it names no path, command, agent or skill a reader with nothing installed could not resolve. `/brd-package` inlines its body — from section 2 onward, never the whole file — into the prompt the customer pastes, and `/brd-reconcile` parses a returned review against that same body.

## Shared by both routes

The one rule the BRD route and the idea ladder both execute.

- `linked-sources.md` — how a markdown document's links are found, resolved and walked, once for `/brd-intake` and `/idea`: the four link forms (the `[[wikilink]]` among them), the vault-aware resolution that reports an ambiguous wikilink rather than guessing between two files, the read-only breadth-first walk and the record it produces, and the rule that a file is taken only where a chain of taken markdown reaches it.

## PRD-ladder formats

The canonical structure each artifact of the idea → PRD → ARD → specification ladder is authored and reviewed against.

- `idea-format.md` — canonical structure and per-section rules for a refined idea brief; `/idea` is the only author and cites it directly, while `/create-prd` consumes the resulting artifact without citing this format doc. It also owns the *Vendored sources* rules `/idea` Phase 4.5 executes: text and markdown sources copied into the folder's reserved `attachments/`, read images into `design/idea-sources/`, the `_NN` collision suffix that is never compounded, and the rule that a link in the brief is repointed only where the target was actually copied. `/brd-intake` Phase 2 is its second consumer: it executes the collision rule's rules 1–3, by number, for the files a BRD links from outside its own folder, and never rule 4, which would overwrite a copy. The index that frame set must carry is **not** this file's — the grounding-format reference in `workflows-core` requires it in its §6.1 and formats it in its §6.2, and this file states only what `/idea` contributes to a row.
- `ard-format.md` — canonical structure and rules for an Architecture Requirements/Decision Document — **ard.md**, or **ard-\<area\>.md** for an area-scoped one — plus its `kind:`/`key:` frontmatter; `ard-reviewer` reviews against it and `dev-workflows:ready` reads its `grounded_repos:` frontmatter. At PRD level it also carries `components:` — the components the PRD touches — and, with two or more, a `## Contracts` section fixing the interfaces between them, which `/epics`, `/specify`, `/design`, `/ready` and `/implement` read.
- `specification-format.md` — canonical structure and per-stage rules for a product specification — **specification.md** — plus its `kind:`/`key:` frontmatter; `/specify` authors against it and `spec-reviewer` reviews against it. An embedded snapshot, not a net-new format.

## Effort-proposal format

The canonical structure of the two customer-facing estimate artifacts, and the rules a reviewer checks them against.

- `proposal-format.md` — the section set of **proposal.md** and its derived **proposal-brief.md**, the `[WP#n]` work-package and `[ED#n]` estimate-driver namespaces, the four readiness tiers and the ceiling each puts on per-package confidence, the three confidence grades with their default ranges and the tiered severity of a deviation from one, the closed three-class set of evidence a cost driver may cite, the rule that a defect-remediation package is never a scope lever, the range's own exclusions as a section distinct from exclusions-from-scope, the rule that a requirement is cited in the form its source artifact carries it — and why that puts these two artifacts deliberately outside `workflows-core:pre-lint`'s auto-link collision check — and the correction-versus-re-estimate classification a revision's changelog computes. `/prd-proposal` and `/brd-proposal` author against it and `proposal-reviewer` reviews against it.

Every one of the 12 files above is cited by at least one command or agent. The engineering-design counterpart one altitude down — the format an implementation design is authored against — ships in the companion `dev-workflows` plugin and is not part of this one.
